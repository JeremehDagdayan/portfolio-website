Explore the Maps

Valorant maps are built for strategy and creativity. Learn the layout and best playstyles for each location.

Ascent

A Venice-inspired map with a mid-control focus and destructible doors.

Map Quirk

Ascent features closeable doors, which can be used to block off certain areas and create opportunities for strategic plays. The central open area, called Mid, allows for a lot of action and offers verticality with the ability to control high ground.

Breeze

Large open spaces with long sightlines, ideal for snipers and flanks.

Map Quirk

Breeze is known for its wide open spaces, creating opportunities for long-range combat and strategic plays. The open sites and elevated positions give attackers and defenders different tactical options when engaging.

Sunset

A classic two-site map with narrow alleys and vibrant Los Angeles vibes.

Map Quirk

Sunset features wide open spaces and long sightlines, which encourage long-range combat and sniper duels, particularly around areas like A Site. The rotating door near B Site creates a dynamic element, offering opportunities for both attackers and defenders to rotate quickly, but also making it a key point for strategic control.
