Meet the Agents

Each agent in Valorant brings unique abilities and playstyles to the battlefield.

Jett

An agile duelist with explosive mobility and quick reflexes.

Agent Signature Abilities

Jett’s signature ability is Tailwind, which allows her to dash a short distance in any direction. This ability is rechargable if you get two kills. Tailwind enabling her to quickly reposition herself during a fight or evade danger.

Sova

A recon expert who tracks, reveals, and eliminates enemies from afar.

Agent Signature Abilities

Sova’s signature ability is Recon Bolt, which allows him to fire an arrow equipped with a sonar pulse that reveals enemy positions within its radius. When the bolt hits a surface, it deploys a scanning field, showing the location of any enemies caught within it.

Killjoy

A master of tech and traps, she locks down zones with precision.

Agent Signature Abilities

Killjoy’s signature ability is Turret, which deploys a small automated turret that automatically detects and fires at enemies within its range. The turret doesn’t do much damage but serves as a valuable tool for gathering information about enemy positions and slowing them down.
